This Data Security Addendum (“Addendum”) supplements the Master Services Agreement between the parties and governs the processing and protection of Customer Data (including voter, volunteer, and campaign data) handled by the Nirvachak AI platform. In case of conflict with the Agreement, this Addendum controls with respect to data security matters.

1. Definitions

Customer Data means any personal or operational data submitted by, or generated on behalf of, Customer through the Platform. Personal Data, Processing, Data Controller, and Data Processor have the meanings given under applicable data protection laws (including the DPDP Act, 2023 and, where applicable, the GDPR).

2. Roles & Scope

Customer is the Data Controller/Fiduciary of Customer Data. Provider acts as Data Processor/Data Processor-equivalent, Processing Customer Data solely to deliver the Services and per Customer’s documented instructions.

3. Security Program

Provider maintains a written information security program aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 (Security) principles, reviewed at least annually and covering governance, risk management, and personnel security.

4. Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256) for all Customer Data stores and backups.
  • Role-based access control, least-privilege enforcement, and MFA for administrative access.
  • Segregation of production, staging, and development environments; no production data in non-production.
  • Centralized logging, tamper-evident audit trails (see AuditLog), and 12-month retention minimum.
  • Vulnerability scanning (monthly) and independent penetration testing (annual).
  • Secure SDLC: code review, dependency scanning, and secrets management.

5. Organizational Safeguards

  • Background checks and confidentiality agreements for all personnel with data access.
  • Mandatory annual security & privacy training.
  • Documented access provisioning, quarterly access reviews, and prompt de-provisioning on role change or exit.

6. Hosting & Data Residency

Customer Data is hosted in [Region, e.g., India (Mumbai/Hyderabad)]. Cross-border transfer, if any, occurs only under lawful transfer mechanisms and with Customer’s prior written consent.

7. Sub-processors

Provider may engage vetted sub-processors (e.g., cloud hosting, email/SMS gateways, telephony) under written contracts imposing equivalent obligations. A current list is available on request; Customer will receive at least 30 days’ notice of material changes.

8. Incident Response

Provider will notify Customer of a confirmed Security Incident affecting Customer Data without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after confirmation, including known scope, impact, and remediation steps. Provider will reasonably cooperate with Customer’s statutory reporting obligations.

9. Business Continuity

Encrypted backups performed daily; documented RPO ? 24 hours and RTO ? 24 hours for the production environment. Disaster recovery procedures are tested at least annually.

10. Data Subject Rights

Provider will assist Customer, at Customer’s reasonable request, in responding to data-principal requests for access, correction, erasure, and portability, using Platform features where available.

11. Retention & Deletion

Customer Data is retained for the term of the Agreement and up to [30] days thereafter to permit export, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized within a further [60] days, subject to legal-hold requirements.

12. Audit

Provider will make available summaries of third-party audit reports (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001) once per year under NDA. Where legally required, Customer may conduct a proportionate on-site audit on reasonable prior notice, at Customer’s cost.

13. Confidentiality & Compliance

All Customer Data is treated as Confidential Information. Provider complies with applicable laws including the DPDP Act, 2023 and the IT Act, 2000 and applicable Election Commission of India guidance regarding voter information handling.
