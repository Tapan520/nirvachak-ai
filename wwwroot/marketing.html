Platform Features

Everything Your Campaign Needs

From first voter visit to result day — one platform replaces your WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, and manual booth reports.

🗺

Voter Intelligence

Track Favour, Against, Neutral, Floating sentiment for every voter. Filter by booth, ward, gender. Identify swing voters instantly.

🏆

Field Leaderboard

Real-time worker performance ranking. Score = Visits x2 + Calls x1 + Favour Conversions x3. Motivate your team daily.

📊

AI Win Probability

Live win score calculated from voter sentiment, booth coverage, floating voter potential and contact rate. Know where you stand every day.

🕵

Competitor Intelligence

Log competitor rallies, door-to-door campaigns, social media threats. Track threat levels (Low to Critical). Plan your counter-strategy.

💰

EC Expense Compliance

Track every rupee against the Rs.40L EC cap. Auto-generate EC-compliant PDF expense reports ready for submission.

📞

Phone Banking

Log every voter call. Track outcomes (Talked, No Answer, Call Back). Update sentiment after call. Full call history per voter.

🔽

Panna Pramukh Tracker

Manage Panna Pramukhs booth-wise and panna-wise. Track voters assigned vs contacted. Monitor coverage in real-time.

🚀

Election Day Live Hub

Real-time booth-wise voter turnout via SignalR. Mark voters as Voted / Absent. Coordinate transport for voters who need pickup.
